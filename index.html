    How to play

    1. 1Start on the glowing tile and drag the snake through every tile on the board. Up, down, left or right. No diagonals.
    2. 2Every 5 letters must spell a word. All five words fit today's theme.
    3. 3Boxed yourself in? Drag back, tap an earlier tile, or press Undo to rewind.
    4. 4Fill all 25 tiles to finish. Your time is your score, and each hint adds 20 seconds.

    The snake spells TRACE, turns the corner, and spells WORDS.
